Meet Hany

24+ years serving our community

For more than 24 years, Hany has driven people around New Providence and the surrounding towns. Many of his riders are his neighbors, and he drives them the way he'd drive his own family.

Hany can’t be behind the wheel for every ride. When someone else comes for you, it’s a driver he knows personally and chose himself — held to the same standard, or they don’t drive for us.

Getting you there safely is the baseline. Making you feel looked after is the standard we hold ourselves to.

We remember how you travel — your cabin temperature, the car seat, the Suburban or the sedan. Set from the second ride on.

Read Hany's story

I came to America from Egypt looking for an opportunity to build a better life, and work that meant something to me. Along the way I realized what makes me happiest isn’t doing a job — it’s serving people, treating them fairly, and knowing someone had a better day because of how I treated them.

That’s the idea behind The Standard.

We’re not trying to get more customers. We want to build real relationships with the people we serve.

A great ride isn’t about a beautiful car, or how everything looks from the outside. It’s about how you feel on the inside — comfortable, safe, respected, genuinely cared for.

We want you to arrive feeling you weren’t just another reservation on our schedule. You were someone we were happy to serve.

That is our standard.
